Terms of use

The rules for using Worthing Brighton Bus, in plain words. By using the site you accept them. If you don't, please don't use it.

Last updated 15 September 2026.

Who runs it

Worthing Brighton Bus is an independent volunteer project run by Connor R. It is not run by, funded by or endorsed by any bus operator, train operator or council. Contact: privacy@worthingbrightonbus.co.uk.

The information is a guide, not a guarantee

Live times, timetables, fares, ticket zones and journey results come from published data that can be late, incomplete or wrong, and from calculations this site makes with it. Buses and trains run late, change and are cancelled. Before a journey that matters, particularly the last bus or train of the day, check with the operator. Fares and ticket rules are set by the operators and can change without this site knowing.

The service is free and provided as it is. It may be unavailable, slow or changed at any time, for example while its free hosting wakes up.

Campaign content

Parts of the site argue for better bus services. Those arguments are opinions, based on the published data and sources they cite, and each figure says where it came from and when. If something is wrong, please say so (see About and corrections) and it will be put right.

Sending something in

When you send an idea, a route, news or a report, you confirm that:

  • it is yours to share, and as accurate as you can make it;
  • it does not include anyone else's personal details, such as names of drivers or members of the public, photos of people, or contact details;
  • it is not defamatory, abusive, discriminatory, or otherwise unlawful, and does not advertise anything.

Submissions are reviewed before anything is published, and publishing is not guaranteed. What is published may be shortened, edited for clarity or safety, or removed later. By sending something in, you allow it to be published on this site, with the name you give, under the same licence as the rest of the site's content (below). You keep any rights you have in it. You can ask for it to be removed at any time. How submissions are handled is set out in the privacy notice.

Reporting content and complaints

Illegal content, and content harmful to children, is not allowed anywhere on this site. That includes hate, threats, harassment, abuse, encouragement of suicide or self-harm, and anything sexual. Every submission is read by a person before anything is published, and anything like that is not published.

If you see something on the site you think is illegal or harmful, or you want to complain about a decision (for example that something you sent was not published, or was removed), or about how the site meets its online safety duties, email privacy@worthingbrightonbus.co.uk and say which page and what the problem is. You will get a reply. Content found to be illegal is removed as soon as it is found, and a decision you disagree with will be looked at again.
