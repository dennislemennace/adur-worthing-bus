# Draft: a request to the operators for wheelchair ramp data

For the maintainer to send (not sent by anyone yet). Written 6 November 2026,
from docs/POST_EXPO_ROADMAP.md ("Ramp use at a stop"). To Brighton & Hove
Buses, Stagecoach South and Compass Travel, copied to the Enhanced Partnership
officers at West Sussex County Council and Brighton & Hove City Council.

---

**Subject: Wheelchair ramp use by stop, for the Bus Service Improvement Plan**

Dear [name],

I run worthingbrightonbus.co.uk, an independent site that shows live buses,
departure boards and measured performance along the coast from Worthing to
Brighton, built from the Bus Open Data Service.

Passengers who use wheelchairs have asked which stops see the ramp used most,
and where boarding with a ramp is hardest. The open location feeds carry no
ramp or door status, so we cannot answer that from them. Riders now log ramp
use by hand on the site, but that only covers the buses they happen to ride.

Many buses record ramp deployment through their telematics or ticket machines.
Would you be willing to share, through the Enhanced Partnership:

- the number of ramp deployments by stop (NaPTAN ATCO code), by month, for the
  last twelve months, for the routes in Adur, Worthing and Brighton & Hove;
- if recorded, the typical time the ramp is out at each stop.

We would publish only what you agree to, credited to you, with your caveats,
beside the stop's access details (step-free access, kerb, shelter) that the
site already shows from OpenStreetMap. The aim is evidence for the Bus Service
Improvement Plan: where raised kerbs, clearer stops or more time in the
timetable would help most.

Happy to talk it through, and to share how the site works first.

Yours sincerely,

Connor Ranahan
privacy@worthingbrightonbus.co.uk
