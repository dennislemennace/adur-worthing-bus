About this site

Worthing Brighton Bus shows live buses and trains along the Adur, Worthing and Brighton coast, works out what a journey costs in tickets, and sets out a case for better service across the council boundary, which runs through Portslade.

It is independent. It is not run by, funded by, or endorsed by any bus operator, train operator, or council. Operator names, route numbers and liveries appear here to identify services, and that is all they mean. Where the site quotes a fare or a departure count, it says where the figure came from and when it was checked.

Correcting something

If something here is wrong, please say so. A campaign site that gets its facts wrong is worse than no campaign site. The quickest route is to open an issue on the public issue tracker, which is where the project's work happens in the open. Anything posted there is public, so keep personal details out of it.

To have something you sent removed, or for anything about your personal information, email privacy@worthingbrightonbus.co.uk instead. It will be taken down.

Privacy and terms

The site sets no cookies, counts visits without them (and you can switch that off), and keeps submissions private until a person has reviewed them. The privacy notice sets out exactly what happens to information about you, who is responsible and your rights. The terms of use cover what the information is and is not, the rules for submissions, and reuse.

Accessibility

The site aims at WCAG 2.2 AA, with 44 px targets and AAA text contrast where it can manage them. Known gaps, stated rather than hidden: the map itself is difficult to operate without a pointer, which is why the live view carries a stop and station search that does the same job from the keyboard. Text size, reduced motion and a colour-blind-safe palette can be changed in the site's accessibility settings, which are also linked from the footer of every page. If something is unusable for you, that is a bug. Please report it on the tracker, or by email, and say what you were trying to do.

Postcode lookups
postcodes.io, using the ONS Postcode Directory. Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0. Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2026. Contains Royal Mail data © Royal Mail copyright and database right 2026.
Councillors
Names and contact details come from each council's own published member directory and are never derived from a name pattern.

Service comparisons on this site count scheduled departures from the published timetable. That is not the same as buses that actually ran, and a difference in service either side of a boundary is not on its own proof that the boundary caused it. The full method, denominators and caveats are published with the figures.
