Contact

Worthing Brighton Bus is run by one volunteer. Email is the best way to get in touch, and every message is read by a person.

Email

privacy@worthingbrightonbus.co.uk

For anything about your personal information, including a request to see it or have it deleted, and for anything else you would rather not post in public. You should get a reply within a few days, and within one month for a request about your information.

Something on the site is wrong

A wrong fare, a stop in the wrong place, a figure that does not add up: the About and corrections page says how to report it and how corrections are made. Email works too.

An idea for the buses

Ideas to improve the network go through the form under Better buses, Your ideas. No account is needed, and a person reads each one before anything is published.

Bus times, lost property and complaints about a journey

This site is independent and does not run any buses. For those, contact the bus company: Brighton & Hove Buses, Stagecoach, Compass Travel or Metrobus.
