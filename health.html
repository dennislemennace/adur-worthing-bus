Live data health

How well each operator's buses can be followed, and how often the live times on the board were right, judged by the measures the Real Time Information Group (RTIG) recommends. The board was rebuilt minute by minute from the recorded feeds and checked against when each bus really left. Updated every night; every share gives what it was divided by.

From 3 November 2026 the board passes on four fifths of a late bus's lateness rather than all of it, so readers are more likely to be at the stop early than to miss the bus. On replayed days that cut how often a bus came before the time shown from 5.4% to 3.4% of estimates, and those within RTIG's window from 77.3% to 72.0%. Days before then were scored as the board showed them at the time; the 30-day figures move over in full by 3 December.
