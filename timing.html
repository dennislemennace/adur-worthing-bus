How to time a bus
  1. Choose the stop pole you are standing at: nearest, or search.
  2. As a bus pulls in, type its route and the fleet number on the back or side (Stagecoach, Brighton & Hove, Metrobus), or its registration (Compass).
  3. Tap Doors open when they open, and Bus left the moment it pulls away. If you missed the doors, just tap Bus left.
  4. Fix any typing in the list below; it saves as you go. Export at the end of the session.
