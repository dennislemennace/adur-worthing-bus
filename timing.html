How to time a bus
  1. Choose the stop pole you are standing at: nearest, or search. The pole across the road is set for you as the other side, so you can time buses both ways: tap L or R, just above the timing buttons, to switch before you tap a bus.
  2. As a bus pulls in, tap its route (or type it). That is all most buses need: each is matched to the bus of that route we recorded at this pole.
  3. If two buses of the same route are at the stop together, also type the registration plate of the one you are timing.
  4. Tap Doors open when they open, and Bus left the moment it pulls away. If you missed the doors, just tap Bus left.
  5. A bus still at the other pole can be finished from the list with Bus left now.
  6. Fix any typing in the list below; it saves as you go. Export at the end of the session.
